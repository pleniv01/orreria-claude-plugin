# Orreria connector privacy notice

Effective October 19, 2026.

The public, versioned notice is [Orreria connector privacy](https://orreria.com/connector/privacy.html). It explains necessary place queries and connection metadata, transient caches, hosting logs, upstream geocoding/weather/ecology providers, retention and support requests.

The connector does not request personal reflections, account records, conversation history, memory or files. No readings are saved to public Field Notes. The initial server configuration makes no generative-model calls. Named geocoding queries may be cached in memory for reuse for 24 hours; reading/place identifiers expire after 15 minutes. Expired entries are cleared on later requests or restart. Provider infrastructure retention is separate; this is not a zero-retention service.

Contact: support@orreria.com. See the public notice for full details and provider policy links.
